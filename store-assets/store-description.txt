• קאפיר תגובה לינק – א קליק אויפן קליינעם אייקאן נעבן דעם נאמען פונעם שרייבער.

★ נאטיפיקאציעס

• א @דערמאנונג אין א תגובה איז א לינק צום באנוצער'ס פראפייל.

iVelt Helper – shortcuts, notifications and other useful things for the אייוועלט (ivelt.com) forum.
Most features can be turned on or off in the extension's settings, and they stay the same on all your computers.

★ Post buttons
• Quote last – for a post with quotes in it, quote only the last reply.
• Quote selected words – select words in a post and quote only them.
• Mention – the poster gets a notification.
• Quote in another topic – with a link to the source, and the images if you want.
• Quote in the comments topic – opens a reply there with the quote already in it.
• Copy post link – one click on the small icon next to the author's name.
• Who liked – a small arrow on the like button shows who liked the post.
• Double-click a post to like it.
• Profile card – rest the mouse on a name to see their profile.

★ Notifications
• Unread count on the toolbar icon, and browser notifications, even when אייוועלט isn't open.
• An alert when new replies arrive in the topic you're reading.
• Separate notification bells for quotes, likes and mentions, with a grey "+N" for older unread ones.
• An @mention in a post links to that user's profile.

★ Writing a post
• Drafts – what you type is kept, so a closed tab or a crash doesn't lose it.
• Undo (Ctrl+Z) and redo (Ctrl+Y) while writing.
• Image resizer: shrinks images to fit the attachment size, and packs a whole folder into one ZIP.
• Paste an image straight into a post.
• Upload files to your own Google Drive or Dropbox and insert the link in the post, in private messages too. Drag files straight onto the button, and optionally a video dragged into the post goes there too.
• A warning before leaving a page with an unsent post.

★ Keyboard shortcuts
• ← / → – previous / next page in a topic
• ↑ / ↓ – top / bottom of the page
• Ctrl+Enter – send the post  •  Alt+V – preview
• Alt+A – active topics  •  Alt+N – notifications  •  Alt+M – next unread notification

★ Reading
• Clicking a topic with new replies opens it at the first unread post.
• Nicer spoilers: words and pictures stay covered in place until clicked.
• 16 color themes, light and dark.

★ More
• Google search in the topic, or across all of אייוועלט.
• Print several pages or a whole topic at once, optionally with who liked each post.
• Toolbar popup with quick links, quick sign-in and emergency sign-out.
